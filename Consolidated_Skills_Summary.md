### Core Principles Applied

All new skills follow these rules from the source files:

- **Single Responsibility**: Each skill solves ONE highly specific problem
- **Concrete Triggers**: 8-12 natural language phrases per skill
- **Discoverability**: Clear descriptions for routing

### Developer Workflow
- **Systematic Debugging**: Mandatory root-cause analysis steps
- **TDD Enforcement**: Test-first architectural patterns

### Knowledge Management
- **Structured Knowledge**: Tapestry-style graph building
- **Document Ingestion**: EPUB, PDF, article extraction

### Quality Standards
- **Concise**: Under 500 lines where possible
- **Specific**: No vague descriptions
- **Referenced**: Links to origin repositories

### What Works vs. What Doesn't

#### What Works
- Skills solving ONE specific problem really well
- Dev-focused skills have highest quality
- Hooks are powerful if you invest time learning them
- Documentation-to-skill generators are most useful

#### What Doesn't Work
- Vague skills that try to do too much
- Complicated setup that breaks on updates
