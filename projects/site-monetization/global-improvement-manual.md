# Global Improvement Manual — Future Roadmap

A living reference for growing sourovdeb.com after the AdSense blockers are cleared. Reviewed quarterly. Sequenced from highest-leverage to nice-to-have.

---

## Guiding principle

**Free content, ad-supported, credible.** Every decision serves:
1. **More qualified traffic** (SEO + sharing)
2. **Better ad revenue per visitor** (placement + relevance, never at the cost of trust)
3. **Stronger authority** (so advertisers and Google trust the site)

---

## 1. SEO — the biggest lever (ongoing)

**Quarter 1 priorities**
- **Meta everywhere:** every post gets a unique SEO title + description. Start with your 20 most-visited posts.
- **Internal linking:** each post links to 2–3 related posts in the body. Build "hub" posts that link out to a cluster.
- **Fix thin/duplicate posts:** merge or expand any post under ~300 words.
- **XML sitemap:** confirm Yoast/Rank Math is generating one; submit in **Google Search Console**.
- **Schema markup:** add LearningResource/Course schema to ELT posts; Article schema site-wide.

---

## 2. Content structure & navigation

- **Rename "Blog"** to something specific ("Essays" or "Notes").
- **Promote ELT Masterclass** to a top-level menu item.
- **Category cleanup:** clear hierarchy, review yearly.
- **Featured images:** add one per post going forward.

---

## 3. Monetization maturity

**Phase 1 — AdSense (now):** get approved, run tasteful auto/manual ads.

**Phase 2 — optimise (3–6 months):**
- Watch **RPM** in AdSense. Block low-quality ad categories.
- ELT/IELTS keywords carry decent European CPCs.

**Phase 3 — diversify (6–12 months):**
- **Affiliate links** — books (Amazon), DxO software. Disclose them.
- **Digital products** — paid ELT resource pack or course; blog stays free.
- **Ko-fi** — voluntary reader support.

**Keep the promise:** the *blog* stays free.

---

## 4. Legal / compliance upkeep

- **Formal non-profit status:** *association loi 1901* in France — separate legal step, outside this package.
- **Affiliate disclosure:** required by law once you add affiliate links.
- **Keep Privacy Policy current:** update whenever you add a new data-collecting tool.
- **Email list:** if you start one, explicit opt-in + unsubscribe required (GDPR). Use Mailchimp or Brevo.
- **Accessibility:** readable contrast, alt text on images, descriptive links.

---

## 5. Performance & technical health

- **Core Web Vitals:** check in Search Console. Use LiteSpeed Cache or WP Super Cache.
- **Image optimisation:** ShortPixel/Smush — biggest speed win.
- **Mobile-first:** most traffic and ad revenue is mobile.
- **Broken link check:** run quarterly (Broken Link Checker plugin).
- **Backups:** confirm Hostinger backups are on, or add UpdraftPlus.

---

## 6. Analytics & the monthly review loop

30-minute monthly review:
1. Top 10 posts by traffic — can any be updated?
2. Top queries in Search Console — any at positions 5–15?
3. AdSense RPM & top-earning pages.
4. Bounce/engagement — weak intros and slow pages are usual culprits.
5. One experiment — change one thing; measure next month.

Log in `microblog/metrics.md`.

---

## 7. Consistency engine

- **Editorial calendar:** 1–2 quality posts/month minimum.
- **Repurpose:** turn strong posts into social snippets or newsletter issues.
- **Batch:** draft several posts in one sitting, schedule them out.

---

## Quarterly review checklist

- [ ] Meta titles/descriptions added to next batch of top posts
- [ ] Internal links added to recent posts
- [ ] Search Console: reviewed top queries, optimised 3 near-page-1 posts
- [ ] AdSense: checked RPM, adjusted one placement
- [ ] Privacy Policy still accurate
- [ ] Site speed / Core Web Vitals checked
- [ ] Backups confirmed working
- [ ] One growth experiment run + logged
