# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [[LAST UPDATED]]

This Privacy Policy explains how sourovdeb.com ("the Site") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you visit. We comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and French data protection law.

## 1. Who we are (Data Controller)

The data controller is **[[SITE OWNER NAME]]**, based in **[[LOCATION]]**. For privacy questions, contact us at **[[CONTACT EMAIL]]**.

## 2. What data we collect

- **Information you give us voluntarily** — name and email if you use a contact form or leave a comment.
- **Technical data collected automatically** — IP address (anonymised where possible), browser type, device type, pages visited.
- **Cookies and similar technologies** — see Section 6.

We do **not** knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

## 3. How we use your data

Only to: respond to messages; display and moderate comments; understand site usage; serve and measure advertising; keep the site secure.

## 4. Legal basis for processing (GDPR Article 6)

- **Consent** — for non-essential cookies, analytics, and personalised advertising.
- **Legitimate interest** — for basic security and aggregated usage statistics.
- **Contract / legitimate interest** — to reply when you contact us.

## 5. How long we keep your data

- Contact messages: kept only as long as needed to handle your request.
- Comments: kept while the post is published.
- Analytics data: retained per the analytics provider's default retention.

## 6. Cookies

- **Essential cookies** — required for the Site to function (no consent needed).
- **Analytics cookies** — help us understand traffic (consent required).
- **Advertising cookies** — used by Google AdSense and partners (consent required).

A **cookie consent banner** lets you accept or reject non-essential cookies on first visit.

## 7. Advertising — Google AdSense

This Site displays ads through **Google AdSense** to keep all content free to read. Google and partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits. You may opt out at **adssettings.google.com** or **www.aboutads.info**.

## 8. Third-party services

This Site may use: Google Analytics, WordPress / Hostinger (server logs), embedded content (YouTube, images). Each third party has its own privacy policy.

## 9. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to: access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to processing of your data; data portability; withdraw consent; lodge a complaint with the **CNIL** (www.cnil.fr).

To exercise any right, email **[[CONTACT EMAIL]]**. We will respond within one month.

## 10. Data security

We use HTTPS encryption, access controls, and up-to-date software. No method of internet transmission is 100% secure.

## 11. International transfers

Some providers (e.g. Google) may process data outside the EU under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 12. Changes to this policy

The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be flagged on the Site.

## 13. Contact

Questions? Email **[[CONTACT EMAIL]]**.
