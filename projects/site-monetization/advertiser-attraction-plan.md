# Advertiser Attraction Plan — Step by Step

**Goal:** Make sourovdeb.com earn ad revenue reliably, with content staying free.
**Written for:** working alone, limited energy, small steps.

---

## The honest part (read once)

There is **no guaranteed income** from a website. What CAN be guaranteed is the **process**:

| Monthly visitors | Realistic AdSense income (ELT niche, EU traffic) |
|---|---|
| 1,000 | €2–10 / month |
| 10,000 | €20–100 / month |
| 50,000 | €100–500 / month |
| 100,000+ | €300–1,500 / month + direct sponsor potential |

**The lever is traffic, not ads.** Your 800+ posts are a real asset. Expect 3–6 months before meaningful income, 12+ months to something steady.

---

## How advertisers find a site

1. **Ad networks (AdSense)** — advertisers bid automatically. They "find" you once you're approved and have traffic. → **Phase 1–3**.
2. **Direct sponsors** — companies pay you directly once you have traffic numbers. → **Phase 4**.

Plan: **compliance → approval → traffic → proof → sponsors.**

All documented in `implementation-manual.md` §1–§5.

## Phase 1 — Get measurable (Week 1–2)

1. **Google Search Console** (free): add property → verify.
2. **Submit sitemap:** `sitemap_index.xml` or `sitemap.xml`.
3. **Install Site Kit by Google** (plugin): connects Search Console + Analytics + later AdSense.
4. **Google Analytics 4** via Site Kit — consent-gated by cookie banner.

**Done when:** Search Console shows sitemap "Success."

---

## Phase 2 — Apply to AdSense (Week 2)

Follow `implementation-manual.md` §9. Submit, then **keep publishing while you wait**.

**Done when:** ads render on your posts.

---

## Phase 3 — Traffic engine (Month 1–6, the real work)

### Weekly loop (pick ONE task per session)

1. **Fix metas (15 min/session):** top pages → write SEO title + description.
2. **Build one hub (1 session/week):** "The Complete Guide to X" linking to 8–15 existing posts.
3. **Rescue near-winners (30 min/week):** positions 5–15 in Search Console → improve one.
4. **One new post per week or two** targeting a real search phrase.

### Monthly (30 min)

- Log numbers in `microblog/metrics.md`.
- The weekly audit agent watches for breakage.

**Milestone:** 10,000 visits/month.

---

## Phase 4 — Direct sponsors (after ~10k visits/month)

1. **Media-kit page** (`/advertise/`): audience, monthly visitors, what you offer, prices.
2. **Add "Advertise" to footer.**
3. **Approach 5 relevant companies** by email: ELT publishers, language apps, teacher-training providers.
4. **Always label sponsored content** (legal requirement, EU).

Parallel income once traffic exists: affiliate links, Ko-fi, eventual paid resource pack (blog stays free).
