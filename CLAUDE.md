# Open Bengali Radio — West Bengal Streaming Platform

## Project Summary
Build an open-source or open-platform radio service for West Bengal Bengali-language content. This is a community-driven initiative combining live streaming, on-demand archives, and contributor-hosted content with low-cost, maintainable tech.

## Goals
- Create a sustainable platform for Bengali-language radio content
- Use open-source streaming tools (Airtime, AzuraCast, Liquidsoap)
- Pilot 4–6 week streaming program to validate demand
- Establish community partnerships and content pipeline
- Explore funding (grants, sponsorship, community subscriptions)

## Target Audience
West Bengal residents, Bengali-language diaspora, educators, content creators seeking open platform for community broadcasting.

## Key Initiatives

### 1. Feasibility & Architecture Study
- Compare FM/AM broadcast vs. online streaming (reach, cost, regulatory)
- Evaluate 3 open-source stacks (Airtime, AzuraCast, Liquidsoap)
- Document Bengali localization requirements
- Design hybrid platform: live broadcast + on-demand archive

**Status:** Study to begin week of July 7; due July 10

### 2. Regulatory & Legal
- Map licensing requirements for West Bengal broadcasting
- Identify constraints and timelines for terrestrial radio
- Document compliance path for streaming-only model

**Status:** Research phase

### 3. Pilot Program (4–6 weeks)
- Recruit Bengali content creators & volunteers
- Set up content pipeline (submission, scheduling, QA)
- Define measurement metrics (streams, unique listeners, feedback)
- Launch proof-of-concept stream

**Status:** Planning phase

### 4. Partnerships & Stakeholders
- Map West Bengal community radio orgs, content creators
- Reach out to 5+ potential partners for collaboration
- Build advisory board for guidance on content/technical decisions

**Status:** Stakeholder list in development

### 5. Funding & Budget
- Prepare preliminary budget (hosting, CDN, moderation, curation)
- Identify grant opportunities (nonprofits, media foundations, Indian tech grants)
- Draft sponsorship/subscription model for sustainability

**Status:** Budget framework to be developed
