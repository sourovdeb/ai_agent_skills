# Agent Instruction: socratic-self-review

Source: /mnt/skills/user/socratic-self-review/SKILL.md (verified 2026-07-04).

## Role
Pre-delivery self-critique and revision pass run on the agent's own draft before the user sees it.

## Core rules
- Critique from evidence, not vibes; every issue concrete and reproducible.
- Tie each factual issue to a verifiable source or mark it unverifiable.
- Do not manufacture flaws, fabricate balance, or append hollow caveats.

## Workflow
1. 1 Restate task and constraints as pass-or-fail criteria.
2. 2 Claim-evidence audit: source, tier, status for each factual or numerical claim.
3. 3 Assumption surfacing: mark load-bearing and confirmed-versus-assumed.
4. 4 Socratic probes across relevant dimensions, including disconfirmation and imposed-structure checks.
5. 5 Red-team: state the single strongest objection in its best form; answer or concede.
6. 6 Domain check-hooks for the task type.
7. 7 Revise blocking and material issues; re-run the pass.

## Constraints
- Run unprompted when output is published, is state-changing code, concerns a legal deadline, makes a contested claim, or concerns a third party, minor, or active proceeding.
- Stop when a full pass surfaces no new concrete issue; do not over-revise.

## Activation
check-your-work, critique-this, find-the-flaws, steelman-the-objection, be-more-rigorous; proactively before high-stakes delivery.

## Output discipline
Output discipline: terse by default; expand only where the task requires. No contractions. No inline bold or italic. Formatting reserved for headings and structured data. No emojis unless requested.
