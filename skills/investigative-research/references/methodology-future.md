# Investigative Research Methodology v2 — Future Protocol

Successor to `methodology.md`, designed for the environment investigations now operate in: live search available by default, AI-generated content contaminating every tier, and sources that change or vanish after citation. v1 remains valid; v2 adds the layers v1 could not foresee. Use v2 whenever live web access exists; fall back to v1 disclosure rules when it does not.

## 1. Provenance chain, not just source tier

v1 ranks sources; v2 additionally requires each load-bearing claim to carry a chain: origin record → who republished it → what changed in transit. A tier-2 systematic review citing a retracted tier-3 study inherits the retraction. Minimum chain for a headline claim: two links verified.

## 2. AI-contamination screen

Before a source enters any tier:
- Check whether the outlet discloses AI authorship or has a documented history of undisclosed synthetic content.
- Treat quotes, statistics, and citations found only in aggregator or SEO-farm pages as unverified regardless of how many pages repeat them — replication of text is not replication of evidence.
- Prefer sources with named human authors carrying professional accountability (byline history, institutional affiliation, correction record).
- A claim whose only trail leads through content published after 2023 with no pre-existing primary record gets the label SYNTHETIC-RISK until a primary record is found.

## 3. Live-verification pass (mandatory when search is available)

1. For each load-bearing claim, attempt to open the primary record itself (registry, filing, dataset), not a description of it.
2. Archive what was found: capture the access date and, where possible, a permanent archive link (Wayback or equivalent) — cited pages change and vanish.
3. Re-run the two most damaging searches with inverted framing ("evidence against X") so the search engine's confirmation loop does not write the piece.
4. Record dead ends in the draft: "searched for Y in registry Z, not found" is a finding.

## 4. Uncertainty grammar

Replace binary confirmed/unconfirmed with four explicit grades, attached inline:
- **VERIFIED-PRIMARY** — the primary record was opened and says this.
- **VERIFIED-SECONDARY** — two independent tier-1/2 sources agree; primary not directly opened.
- **REPORTED** — credible outlet(s) state it; no independent verification achieved.
- **SYNTHETIC-RISK / RECOLLECTION-ONLY** — trail insufficient; usable only with the label visible.

## 5. Time-decay rule

Every verified claim carries its verification date. Claims about ongoing situations older than 90 days are re-verified before republication; regulatory and legal statuses are re-verified every time. "True when checked" is the honest standard; the date makes it checkable.

## 6. Counter-evidence quota

v1 requires a balance section; v2 sets a floor: at least one search per major finding must be spent actively seeking disconfirmation, and the strongest surviving counter-finding appears in the piece even when weak — labeled with the same grammar. If nothing was found, the search that failed is named.

## 7. Publication gate

A piece ships only when: every headline claim is VERIFIED-PRIMARY or VERIFIED-SECONDARY; every lower-grade claim is visibly labeled; the provenance chain of the lead finding is documented; archive links exist for the three most load-bearing citations; and the self-debate or socratic-self-review pass has run. Otherwise the piece ships as a labeled interim note, not as findings.
