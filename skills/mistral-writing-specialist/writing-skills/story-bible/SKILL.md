# Story Bible and story architecture

Read the supplied brief and relevant existing sections before adding material. Extract rather than invent when the task is organization. Preserve competing ideas in Braindump; an interesting possibility is not canon. Invention is welcome for an explicitly creative request, with additions labelled PROPOSAL outside the finished prose.

Use the seven author-facing sections: Braindump (raw notes, alternatives and questions); Genre (reader promise, audience and boundaries); Style (viewpoint, tense, register, rhythm, imagery and examples); Synopsis (protagonist, pressure, stakes and trajectory); Characters (desire, fear, contradiction, agency, relationships, voice and knowledge); Worldbuilding (rules, costs, institutions, places and exceptions); Outline (scene IDs, viewpoint, place/time, objective, obstacle, turn and consequence).

Maintain four supporting records when relevant: Timeline, Canon, Sources and Session checkpoint. Give reusable facts stable IDs and source locators. Distinguish what is true in the story from what a character believes. Empty fields are unknown, not invitations to fabricate biography or world rules.

For /outline, connect beats through decisions and consequences rather than a list of events. Track promises and payoffs, pressure, available information and the scene's changed state. A useful scene card has: ID; viewpoint; time/place; entering state; intention; resistance; choice/turn; exit state; required canon; unresolved thread. Do not force a twist or a fixed act structure.

For style calibration, infer a provisional style card from author-supplied examples: sentence rhythm, diction, distance, humour, figurative density and dialogue habits. Quote short evidence from those examples and test against a small rewrite. Do not assume the author's style from their name, language or identity. Track negative preferences without turning them into universal bans.

Return only the requested sections or outline, then proposed changes and unresolved contradictions. With local tools, read the revision and stage the complete updated section. Without tools, provide the delta and its base version. Stop after a coherent unit; do not fill the whole book without being asked.
