## Fact Checking Process

### Claim Verification
1. Identify claim
2. Find source
3. Verify source credibility
4. Check for supporting evidence
5. Validate data
6. Cross-reference with other sources
7. Document verification

### Source Evaluation Criteria
- **Authority**: Who is the author/publisher?
- **Accuracy**: Is the information correct?
- **Purpose**: Why was it created?
- **Currency**: Is it up to date?
- **Relevance**: Is it relevant to the topic?

### Plagiarism Detection
- Compare with existing sources
- Check for proper attribution
- Identify uncited sources
- Calculate similarity scores
- Flag potential issues

## Quality Standards

### Content Quality
- **Accuracy**: Factual correctness
- **Clarity**: Easy to understand
- **Completeness**: All necessary information included
- **Consistency**: Uniform style and terminology
- **Originality**: Proper attribution and citation

### Writing Quality
- **Structure**: Logical organization
- **Flow**: Smooth transitions
- **Grammar**: Correct usage
- **Style**: Appropriate tone and voice
- **Conciseness**: No unnecessary words

### Citation Quality
- **Completeness**: All sources cited
- **Accuracy**: Correct citation format
- **Consistency**: Uniform citation style
- **Appropriateness**: Proper use of citations
