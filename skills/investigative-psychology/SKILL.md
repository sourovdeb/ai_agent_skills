# Investigative Psychology Research

A specialized investigative methodology for auditing claims about human psychology, neuroscience, marketing manipulation, and pharmaceutical treatments. Produces citation-grounded micro-blogs with conflict-of-interest analysis, alternative interpretations, and verification disclosures.

## Core Principles

### Zero Hypothesis Rule
State only what documented sources establish. Do not infer motive or intent without explicit evidence. Terms like "hidden" or "suppressed" require documented mechanisms (non-publication records, retractions, sworn disclosures).

### Deep Source Verification
Every source must pass the conflict-of-interest checklist before use. Prioritize:
1. Primary sources (government databases, court documents, original research)
2. Authoritative secondary sources (systematic reviews, investigative journalism)
3. Verified independent sources (non-profit research, whistleblower documentation)

### Balance Requirement
Every piece must include:
- Primary finding with supporting evidence
- Strongest contradictory evidence
- Clear statement of unknowns
- Explicit limitations

## Investigation Domains

### 1. Mental Health & Human Brain
- Neuroscientific evidence on bias, identity, and programming
- Trauma, empathy, and intelligence limits
- Brain plasticity and habit formation
- Conscious vs unconscious processing

### 2. Marketing & Corporate Psychology
- Tactics used by big companies (psychologists, neuroscientists, behaviorists)
- Why these tricks work (cognitive biases, emotional triggers)
- Billions spent on consumer psychology research
- Ethical implications and manipulation mechanisms

### 3. Treatment Comparisons
- Antidepressants vs Therapy: efficacy, side effects, long-term outcomes
- Pharmaceutical vs non-pharmaceutical approaches
- Evidence quality and bias in treatment studies
- Cost-benefit analysis with conflict-of-interest disclosure

### 4. Financial & Political Influences
- Who funds mental health research
- Pharmaceutical company lobbying and influence
- Insurance company interests in treatment choices
- Political agendas in health policy
- Hidden government and big tech data sources

## Workflow

1. Define Investigation Scope
2. Source Gathering from primary databases
3. Conflict-of-Interest Audit
4. Evidence Synthesis
5. Micro-Blog Drafting
6. Automatic Publishing to WordPress and Ghost
7. Continuous Monitoring

## Style Constraints

- Complete grammatically precise sentences
- No contractions, informal language, or emojis
- No inline bold or italic for emphasis
- Formatting only for headings and structured data
- Every factual claim cited to author, year, publication
- Direct links only when verifiable
