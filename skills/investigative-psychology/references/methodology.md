# Investigative Psychology Methodology

## Specialized Sourcing Hierarchy

### Tier 1: Primary Neuroscience & Psychology Sources
- PubMed/MEDLINE
- ClinicalTrials.gov
- PsycINFO
- FDA Drug Approval Packages
- EMA EPAR
- NIH RePORTER
- Cochrane Library
- OpenNeuro
- NeuroVault

### Tier 2: Marketing & Corporate Research
- SEC EDGAR
- EU Transparency Register
- OpenPayments
- USPTO Patent Database
- EPO Espacenet
- FTC Complaints and Actions
- Advertising Standards Authority
- Corporate Annual Reports

### Tier 3: Government & Hidden Sources
- FOIA Requests
- Congressional Hearings
- Whistleblower Portals
- Wayback Machine
- Leaked Document Databases
- Government Research Labs (DARPA, IARPA)
- Intelligence Community Declassified Documents

### Tier 4: Independent & Alternative Sources
- PLOS
- Frontiers
- arXiv/bioRxiv
- Non-profit Research (RAND, Kaiser Family Foundation)
- Investigative Journalism (ProPublica, The Intercept)
- Academic Blogs with verified credentials

## Conflict-of-Interest Checklist

### Funding Analysis
- Grant funding source and amounts
- Corporate sponsorship or partnership
- Foundation funding with potential agenda
- Check OpenPayments for US researchers
- Check EU Transparency Register for European researchers

### Author Affiliations
- Primary employer and department
- Secondary appointments and consulting roles
- Industry advisory board memberships
- Stock ownership in relevant companies
- Patent applications or holdings
- Expert testimony
- Previous employment in industry

### Publication Venue Analysis
- Journal ownership
- Journal funding sources
- Editorial board composition
- Peer review process transparency
- Impact factor manipulation history
- Retraction rate and history

### Research Design Red Flags
- Industry-designed protocol
- Industry-controlled data access
- Industry statistical analysis
- Ghostwriting
- Selective outcome reporting
- Post-hoc hypothesis changes
- Data dredging
- Inappropriate control groups
