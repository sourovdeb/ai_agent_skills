## Search Strategy Guide

### For Mental Health Treatment Comparisons
1. Start with Systematic Reviews (Cochrane, PubMed meta-analyses)
2. Check Clinical Guidelines (NICE, APA, WHO)
3. Review Individual Trials (ClinicalTrials.gov)
4. Examine Real-World Evidence (observational studies)
5. Investigate Funding Sources (OpenPayments, trial registries)

### For Marketing Psychology Tactics
1. Academic Research (PubMed, Google Scholar)
2. Corporate Research (SEC filings, patents)
3. Industry Reports (WARC, AdAge, Nielsen)
4. Regulatory Actions (FTC, ASA)
5. Whistleblower Reports (ProPublica, ICIJ)

### For Neuroscience of Bias and Identity
1. Cognitive Neuroscience (PubMed fMRI studies)
2. Social Neuroscience (social influence, group bias)
3. Developmental Neuroscience (brain development)
4. Clinical Neuroscience (trauma, empathy)
5. Ethical Considerations (neuroethics)
