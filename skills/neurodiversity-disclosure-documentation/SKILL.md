# SKILL: Neurodiversity Disclosure & Documentation
## Formal Disclosure Strategy + Evidence Preservation
**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 29 May 2026 | **Target:** Neurodivergent professionals seeking accommodations

---

## OVERVIEW

Disclosing a neurodivergent diagnosis (ADHD, autism, bipolar, dyslexia, PTSD, etc.) in a professional or educational setting is both a **power move** (triggers legal duty of care) and a **risk** (can trigger discrimination). This skill teaches you to disclose **legally and strategically**.

**What this skill covers:**
- When & how to disclose (timing, channel, documentation)
- What to say & what to avoid
- How to preserve evidence from day one
- How to request formal accommodations
- How to handle refusal or bad faith response

---

## SECTION 1: WHEN & WHY TO DISCLOSE

### Disclose if:
✅ You have a **formal diagnosis** (letter from healthcare provider)  
✅ Your condition **materially affects your work/learning**  
✅ You're seeking **specific accommodations** (extra time, quiet space, flexible schedule)  
✅ You want **legal protection** if things go wrong (creates audit trail)  

### Don't disclose if:
❌ You haven't **seen a professional** (online tests ≠ diagnosis)  
❌ Your condition doesn't **affect this specific context**  
❌ You're **seeking sympathy** (frame as practical need)

## SECTION 2: THE DISCLOSURE LETTER TEMPLATE

```
[YOUR FULL NAME]
[Your address, email, phone]
[Date]

[INSTITUTION/MANAGER NAME & TITLE]
[Institution name and address]

FORMAL DISCLOSURE OF NEURODIVERSITY & ACCOMMODATION REQUEST

Dear [Name/Title],

PURPOSE

I am writing to formally disclose that I have a diagnosis of [CONDITION]
and to request specific accommodations to support my continued [study/work]
at [INSTITUTION/COMPANY].

DIAGNOSIS & CLINICAL CONTEXT

I was diagnosed with [CONDITION] on [DATE] by [HEALTHCARE PROVIDER].

This diagnosis explains [1–2 specific impacts relevant to THIS role]:
  • [Impact 1] → compensated by [strategy]
  • [Impact 2] → affects [specific area]

ACCOMMODATIONS REQUESTED

| Accommodation | Why it helps | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| [Extra time for assignments] | Reduces pressure-induced attention drop | +25% time |
| [Written feedback vs. oral] | Process written communication faster | Email summaries |

LEGAL FRAMEWORK

Under the Equality Act 2010 (UK) / RQTH framework (France), you have a legal duty to:
  • Acknowledge this disclosure within 2 business days
  • Assess reasonable adjustments (at no cost to me)
  • Implement accommodations unless causing undue hardship

NEXT STEPS

I would like to schedule a meeting [within 5 business days] to confirm receipt,
discuss feasible accommodations, and document agreed adjustments in writing.

Please confirm receipt of this letter within 2 business days.

Yours faithfully,
[Your signature]
```

---

## SECTION 3: HOW TO SEND THE LETTER

**DO:**
✅ Email + printed copy  
✅ Read receipt ("Return receipt requested")  
✅ Addressed to specific person  
✅ Save everything (forward to yourself, print email headers)  
✅ Keep tone professional and factual  

**DON'T:**
❌ Casual mention — verbal only doesn't create legal documentation  
❌ Over-share — no full medical history, trauma details, or medication dosages  
❌ Apology tone — frame as practical need, not personal failing

## SECTION 5: WHAT IF THEY REFUSE?

```
Dear [Name],

Thank you for your response regarding my accommodation request.

I note that you have declined [specific accommodation]. I would like to
understand the reason, as per [Equality Act 2010 / local regulation]:

  1. Is this a cost issue? (Reasonable adjustments must be provided at no cost)
  2. Is this a "undue hardship" determination?
  3. Is there a misunderstanding of what I'm requesting?

I'd like to schedule a follow-up meeting within [5 days] to discuss alternatives.

If you're unable to provide a written explanation within [10 business days],
I will escalate this to [HR / union / legal counsel].

Yours faithfully,
[Your name]
```

### Escalation Path:
1. Direct manager/coordinator
2. HR department
3. Disability ombudsman
4. Regulator (Ofqual, ACAS, local authority)
5. Legal representation

## SECTION 6: DIFFERENT NEURODIVERGENCES

| Condition | Focus | Accommodations |
|---|---|---|
| ADHD | Attention, time management, structure | Extra time, written instructions, breaks |
| Autism | Sensory needs, communication style | Quiet workspace, email-first, advance notice |
| Bipolar | Mood episodes, sleep importance | Flexible schedule, predictable routine |
| Dyslexia | Reading/writing speed | Assistive technology, extra time |
| Depression | Energy, concentration, motivation | Flexible start times, manageable workload |

---

## SECTION 7: LEGAL REFERENCES

| Jurisdiction | Law | Key Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| **UK** | Equality Act 2010 | Duty to make reasonable adjustments |
| **EU/France** | GDPR + RQTH | Data protection; mandatory accommodation consultation |
| **USA** | ADA | Reasonable accommodations at no cost to employee |
| **Australia** | Disability Discrimination Act 1992 | Duty to accommodate |
