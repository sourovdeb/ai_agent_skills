## Filling rules

- Segments: one concept each, ordered simplest to most complex; five to eight segments for a 20-30 minute read.
- Visuals: minimum one per segment, chosen from the visual menu in SKILL.md; concrete before abstract; adjacent to the explanation, never in an appendix.
- Worked example: mandatory whenever the topic contains any procedure, rule, or calculation.
- Checks and review: answers must be present; hide them below a quote line or after a break so the reader retrieves first.
- Language: plain sentences; define jargon at first use; for French L1 readers watch false friends and keep sentence length short.
- Factual claims: cite or label. Analogies: label as analogy and state where the analogy breaks.
