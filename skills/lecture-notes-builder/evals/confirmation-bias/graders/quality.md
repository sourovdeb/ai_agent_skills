Pass only if:
- The definition is accurate and understandable to a novice.
- Each major concept has an actual visible visual placed beside its explanation.
- Adult examples are plausible and are not falsely presented as research findings.
- Any analogy is labelled and its limits are stated.
- The writing respects adult intelligence. It uses specific words without jargon overload.
- Research claims are sourced or omitted.
- Checks and review answers are present. At least one question tests application in a new situation.
Fail for decorative diagrams, repeated filler, or an explanation that confuses confirmation bias with simply having an opinion.
